Privacy Policy

Brief & Bill for Android · Effective date: November 4, 2026

In short: Brief & Bill works fully offline. It does not collect, send, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Brief & Bill (“the app”) is published by Favorites Create LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting services. The app does not request any Android permissions and does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

The content you create in the app is saved only in the app’s private storage on your device. This includes:

  • Project names, client names, due dates, status, and stage
  • Checklist tasks and project notes
  • Ideas saved in the Ideas vault

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to any third party.

Clipboard

When you tap an idea to copy it, the app places that text on your device’s clipboard. The app only does this when you ask it to, and it never reads the clipboard.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Opening Settings (the gear icon in the app) and choosing Reset data, or
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage in Android settings.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it if it is deleted. Android device backups, if you have them turned on, are managed by your device and Google, not by us.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your data is stored in the app’s private storage area, which Android protects from other apps. Keep your device secure with a screen lock and system updates.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
